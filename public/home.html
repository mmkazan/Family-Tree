Three steps. No one has to learn anything new.

Built for the relative who will never download an app — and the one who forgot their password.

1

Send a voice note

Anyone in the family WhatsApps Elaia a voice note, photo or message about a relative — a story, a recipe, who's who in an old photo.

2

Elaia translates it — in text and in voice

The original voice is kept exactly as it was. Alongside it: the words in Greek and English side by side, and a spoken version in the other language — so nobody has to be able to read to understand.

3

It lives with the person on your tree

You approve it with one tap, and it appears on that relative's card — with their photos, their places on the map, and everything else the family has shared.

Questions families ask

Does yiayia need to install anything?

No. If she can send a WhatsApp voice note, she can use Elaia. You save Elaia's number in her phone once, and she talks to it like she talks to you.

How good is the translation?

Good enough to understand a story; not a certified translation. The original recording is always kept, and anyone with edit access can correct the text — village dialect and family nicknames included.

My mother struggles to read English — will this work for her?

Yes — that's the point. Every memory the grandchildren leave is spoken back in {Lang}, so she taps play and listens. Nothing to read, nothing to type.

Who can see our tree?

Only people you give a link to. Nothing is public or searchable. Living relatives are hidden on view-only links unless you choose otherwise.

What happens to our memories if we stop paying?

They stay. You keep viewing everything you've added; you just can't add new memories beyond the free 20 until you upgrade again. And you can export the lot at any time.

Which languages does Elaia speak?

Greek, Italian, Polish, Punjabi, Turkish, Portuguese and Spanish — each paired with English, in text and in voice. Greek is where Elaia started. If your family's language isn't here yet, tell us when you sign up; new pairs are added by demand.
